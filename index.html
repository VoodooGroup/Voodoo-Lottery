Test your luck in the Voodoo Lottery, a decentralized lottery where you buy tickets with Voodoo tokens for a chance to double your bet. Players join one of four pools, from Bronze to Diamond, each with its own ticket price and participant limit. When a pool fills, a random winner is chosen and instantly paid double their ticket, and in the Gold and Diamond pools, the extra tokens left over from the total collected are burned, reducing the token supply. After each draw, the pool resets, and all results are fully transparent and recorded on-chain.
